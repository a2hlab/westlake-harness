=== ② where the HOME comes from: OUR installer projection (universal) ===
bms/src/adapter/framework/package-manager/jni/apk_bundle_parser.cpp:61  (MapCategory):
    if (androidCategory == CATEGORY_LAUNCHER)  return "entity.system.home";
apk_bundle_parser.h: "intent-filter.category -> Skills.entities (via MapCategory)".
So at install, every Android launcher activity's CATEGORY_LAUNCHER is projected to OH module.json skill
entity "entity.system.home" -> OH treats the ability as the home/launcher entity -> SceneBoard launches it with
want.action=action.system.home -> the Android intent round-trips to action=MAIN + categories={HOME} (seen in B47-SLA).
This is the INTENDED OH launcher entity (OH apps use entity.system.home for the home-screen ability); the Android-side
CATEGORY_HOME is a reverse-mapping artifact (it should map back to CATEGORY_LAUNCHER, not HOME -- cosmetic mismatch).
APPLIES TO ALL 159 apps -> universal -> cannot discriminate lit from white.
