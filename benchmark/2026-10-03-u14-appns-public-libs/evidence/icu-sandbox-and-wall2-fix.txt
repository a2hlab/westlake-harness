# (1) Where the Android app's /system/usr/icu comes from, and who reads it.

## Provenance
- /system/usr/icu is part of the OHOS base app sandbox (OH mounts /system/usr read-only into every spawned app). Our
  appspawn-x config does not remove it. Proven visible inside the toutiao app sandbox by the crash itself: brk_open's
  scandir("/system/usr/icu") SUCCEEDED and selected version 74 (the fault is the later dlsym/call, not scandir).
- Our Android-app ICU is provided separately: appspawn_x.cfg sets ICU_DATA="/system/android/etc/icu" (icudt72l.dat),
  and the payload ships /system/android/lib64/libicuuc.so + libicui18n.so (ICU 72). So the app has BOTH an ICU-72 data
  dir (/system/android/etc/icu, ours) and an ICU-74 data dir (/system/usr/icu, OH's). tttext hard-codes /system/usr/icu
  -> picks 74 -> mismatches our _72 libs.
- Board facts (outer, read-only 61b): /system/usr/icu = icudt74l.dat; /system/usr/ohos_icu = icudt72l.dat;
  /system/android/etc/icu = icudt72l.dat.

## Who else reads /system/usr/icu
- OUR deployed Android payload libs: NONE (open-site scan of bundle-v3/native/payload/**/*.so for "/system/usr/icu"
  returned nothing). So a per-APP sandbox remap of /system/usr/icu does not disturb our Android payload.
- OH platform libs (/system/lib64 on the board, not in our payload, not scannable locally): OH's own ICU consumers use
  /system/usr/icu (ICU 74). A GLOBAL change to /system/usr/icu would break OH components -> any remap must be
  PER-APP (inside the Android app sandbox only), never global.

# (2) Wall-2 minimal fix options + assessment (version mismatch: tttext wants _74, our Android ICU is _72)

## a) Per-app sandbox: make /system/usr/icu (as seen by the Android app) point at an icudt72l.dat dir
   WHERE: the appspawn-x Android-app sandbox mount config (bind-mount /system/android/etc/icu, or /system/usr/ohos_icu,
          over /system/usr/icu for the android app domain only).
   EFFECT: tttext scandir finds icudt72l -> builds _72 -> matches our libs. No new lib copy (data-dir remap only).
   RISK: must be PER-APP (OH platform in other processes still needs /system/usr/icu=74). Does not help any app that
         hard-codes an expectation of ICU 74 behaviour (none seen). Lowest-risk, smallest blast radius. RECOMMENDED.
   DOUBLE-COPY: none (it is a bind-mount of a data directory, not a library load).

## b) Add _74 alias exports to our Android libicuuc.so / libicui18n.so
   WHERE: rebuild the deployed ICU libs with an alias cohort (ubrk_open_74 -> ubrk_open_72 impl, etc.) or
          U_DISABLE_RENAMING + a _74 alias map.
   EFFECT: dlsym("ubrk_open_74") resolves to the _72 implementation.
   RISK: ICU-72 code answering as ICU-74 -- safe only where the C ABI tttext uses (ubrk_*, u_charType, ...) is
         unchanged 72->74 (it is stable for these, but this is a blanket promise across whatever tttext dlsyms).
         Also must keep the _72 cohort (our own runtime binds _72) -> the lib exports BOTH -> larger symbol table but
         ONE library (no second copy). More invasive than (a); touches a shared artifact used by the whole runtime.
   DOUBLE-COPY: none (same single lib, extra alias symbols).

## c) Other
   c1) Ship ICU-74 Android libs matching OH (rebuild libicuuc/libicui18n at 74 + icudt74l under /system/android/etc/icu):
       aligns everything to 74, but is a full ICU version bump of the Android side (re-binds our runtime's _72 cohort to
       _74 -> ripples through westlake_icu_overrides.cpp kExpectedSuffix "_72" and the whole bind cohort). Biggest blast
       radius. Not minimal.
   c2) Point tttext at /system/android/etc/icu: not possible without modifying the apk (path is hard-coded) -- rejected
       (we do not patch apps).
   RECOMMENDATION: (a) per-app sandbox data-dir remap is the minimal, lowest-risk Wall-2 fix; (b) is the fallback if a
   per-app /system/usr/icu remap is not expressible in the sandbox config.
