   string "AssetManager.mObject cached in runtime and libandroid NDK owner" -> android/lib64/liboh_android_runtime.so
   gAssetManagerOffsets is a per-.so-copy BSS global in BOTH liboh_android_runtime.so (B _ZN7android20gAssetManagerOffsetsE

   runs it (+ that copy's libandroid NDK owner). A runtime/libandroid copy loaded in a different domain is NOT filled.
