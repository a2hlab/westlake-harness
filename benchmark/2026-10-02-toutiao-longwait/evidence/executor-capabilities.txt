### The ONE minimal executor change needed (for whoever owns bms_batch — NOT made here)
  In timed_collection (bms_batch.py ~771), capture maps on each 'shot' event instead of only the first. Smallest diff:
     - replace the guard `if len(rec['screenshots']) == 1:` so the maps block runs for every shot
       (or for shots whose tag is in a small set), still writing maps-<tag>-<pid>.txt per shot.
  It is additive (more maps-*.txt files), touches one site, and does not change screenshot/focus/hilog logic.
  Optional larger alternative: a new `--maps-at 45,90` flag. The one-line guard change is the minimal.
  Until it lands, the window can still run with maps-t20 only (first-shot maps), losing just the t45/t90 maps.
