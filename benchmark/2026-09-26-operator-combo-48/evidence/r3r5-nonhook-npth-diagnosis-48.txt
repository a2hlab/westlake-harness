## Recommendation: whole-library HOLLOW npth stub (metasec-style)
npth's app-facing ABI is structurally tiny — same shape as metasec:
- it exports exactly ONE dynamic symbol: JNI_OnLoad
- NO other APK lib DT_NEEDs libnpth.so (no native-to-native consumer)
- it registers its natives dynamically via RegisterNatives in JNI_OnLoad
So a hollow libnpth.so — JNI_OnLoad returns JNI_VERSION_1_6 and SPAWNS NOTHING (no
worker/monitor thread => the autonomous corruptor is gone) and registers the app's
npth natives as typed-safe no-ops (=> no UnsatisfiedLinkError/NPE when the Java
layer calls them) — kills ALL npth corruption paths at once (hooks, sigaction,
non-hook heap) and cannot regress. The westlake recorder (out-crash42) already
captures crashes, so losing npth's own crash/APM monitoring is acceptable.

Lighter alternative (kept on the table): stay on 7639af00 and neuter only npth's
worker/monitor-thread spawn in JNI_OnLoad. Fewer changes and keeps npth's natives
real, but requires pinning the spawn site (no backtrace) and does not cover any
app-triggered corrupting native. The hollow stub is preferred: comprehensive and
final for npth.
