    Fall back to OH's .z.so spelling when a bare soname does not open
    
    OH names several NDK libraries with a .z.so suffix that Android spells plainly, so an app asking
    for libnativewindow.so gets "No such file or directory" while /vendor/lib64/libnativewindow.z.so
    sits there exporting exactly what was asked for -- ANativeWindow_acquire and the AHardwareBuffer
    family, confirmed by resolving them on the board.
    
    Tried only after the plain name fails, so a board that does ship the Android spelling keeps
    winning, and applied to any caller because the naming is the platform's rather than WebView's.
    
    Worth recording that this did not move the app that exposed it. LocalSend still crashes in
    libflutter.so, and no redirect was logged, so its engine does not reach libnativewindow.so by bare
    name -- the resolver did, which is how the naming gap was found. The gap is real and the fix is
    right; it was not that app's blocker.
