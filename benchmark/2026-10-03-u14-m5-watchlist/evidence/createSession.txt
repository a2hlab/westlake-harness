## M5 mechanism (cc-t3; DIGEST 2026-10-02 line 690) and the fix (07fd530a)
Defect: the bridge binds the Android Dialog (androidType=2 / TYPE_APPLICATION) and the Activity base window
(androidType=1) both as zLevel-0 APP_SUB_WINDOWs of the same MainSession; SceneBoard onSubSessionZLevelChange orders
equal-zLevel sub-windows by creation index, so the FIRST-created dialog gets a LOWER zOrder and is pressed UNDER the
later Activity window (witnessed per key in zorder-proof.txt). In Android the TYPE_APPLICATION dialog should be on top.
Fix 07fd530a (westlake-harness-t3 benchmark/2026-10-02-u14-bridge-r1r3/patches/win-m5-dialog-zlevel.patch), in
oh_window_manager_client.cpp createSession: when androidWindowType==2, property->SetSubWindowZLevel(
DIALOG_SUB_WINDOW_Z_LEVEL = 13000) before CreateAndConnectSpecificSession -- mirroring OH's own client rule
WindowSessionImpl::GetSubWindowZLevelByFlags. Effect: the type-2 dialog renders ABOVE its Activity. Judge the window
by androidType, not the VRI name (dialog and Activity share VRI[<Ability>]).
