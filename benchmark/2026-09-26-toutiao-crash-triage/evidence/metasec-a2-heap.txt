## conclusions
- NOT metasec: fault DSOs are SQLite (via oh_android_runtime) and Mali; neither is libmetasec_ml.so.
- NOT #35 metasec TLS: no pthread/TLS read in libmetasec; r2 has 0 metasec native activity (lazy, #41 holds).
- It is a process-wide musl mallocng heap-metadata corruption (get_meta+92 on __libc_free),
  surfacing in whichever thread free()s a poisoned chunk first (bd_tracker/SQLite, RenderThread/Mali).
- bd_tracker is exactly the '116s bd_tracker SIGSEGV, possibly independent' the outer loop flagged at #38.
- It only appears in r2/r4 because metasec's early exit (r1/r5) or luck (r3) previously hid it:
  removing the sixth exit lets the process live long enough to free a corrupted chunk. Same shape as
  class 5 (fix the earlier crash, the next one surfaces). The corruption is INDEPENDENT of A2/metasec.
