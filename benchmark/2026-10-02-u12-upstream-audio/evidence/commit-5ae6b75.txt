    Bind android.media natives the runtime never registers
    
    MediaPlayer: none of its 49 natives was registered, so its static
    initializer threw and the first class to touch MediaPlayer died with it
    (ntfy during bind; Astro Loop and Doors of Doom before a first frame).
    Ported onto OH's AVPlayer: data sources (served from this process through
    a read callback, since OH's media service could not use the app's
    descriptor; descriptor and URL as fallbacks), asynchronous and blocking
    prepare, play/pause/stop/seek/reset/release, looping, volume, playback
    rate, and state callbacks posted as MediaPlayer events. Android's state
    rules are kept rather than OH's. invoke() answers the track queries every
    prepared player gets; failing them posted MEDIA_ERROR (1, -1010) and
    Doors of Doom left its game. Video output, MediaDataSource, DRM, volume
    shapers and track selection are not ported and answer as AOSP does when
    its player service fails.
    
    AudioTrack: buffer-size natives (MuseKit, via getBufferSizeInFrames) and
    native_set_loop, answered ERROR_INVALID_OPERATION: the shim has no static
    buffer to loop, so a looped sound plays once.
    
    AudioSystem: getParameters/setParameters and ExoPlayer's output
    capability queries (offload, direct playback, direct profiles, HW A/V
    sync, haptics, microphones, Bluetooth offload formats), answered as an
    output without the capability.
    
    Registered from libwl_missing_natives' JNI_OnLoad, before any field or
    method lookup: GetFieldID initializes MediaPlayer, whose initializer
    calls native_init.
