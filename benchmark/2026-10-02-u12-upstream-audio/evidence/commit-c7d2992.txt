    OpenSL ES: continuous buffer-queue fill; dlopen(libOpenSLES) to the adapter
    
    The Android simple buffer queue filled each of OH's pull buffers only
    with what the app had queued when OH asked, then padded with silence.
    OH pulls several times more than a game double-buffers (Godot and Oboe
    use about 1024 frames), so audio played half the time or less: broken,
    off-pitch sound on Ball2Box. Measured with a 1 kHz test tone through the
    adapter and the board's playback capture: 48% (48 kHz) and 73% (44.1
    kHz) of 10 ms windows silent, peak 995-998 Hz. Filling now continues
    after each consumed buffer is reported, since the app refills from that
    callback, and silence is written only when the app stops supplying.
    After: 1000 Hz and no silent windows at either rate, like OHAudio's own
    renderer; Ball2Box's dropouts went from 42% of windows to none.
    
    Oboe (Rhythm Engine) does not link libOpenSLES: it dlopens it and looks
    up slCreateEngine and the SL_IID_* by handle, reaching OH's library past
    the adapter; OH refused its engine interface and the app died. An app's
    dlopen of libOpenSLES.so now returns the shim's own handle. The shim
    gains a real dependency on OH's libOpenSLES, so a lookup by that handle
    finds the adapter's slCreateEngine and Android's IDs first and every
    other OpenSL symbol in OH's library behind them. The adapter's own
    dlopen, from inside the shim, still gets OH's library.
