    Translate Android's OpenSL ES ABI onto OH's
    
    OH's OpenSL ES headers define sl_uint32_t as unsigned long, so on arm64
    SLuint32, SLboolean, SLresult and SLmillisecond are 8 bytes; Android's are
    4. OH reads an Android app's SLDataFormat_PCM with fused fields ("channel
    count not supported"), a buffer-queue locator's count as 0, and required[]
    arrays at the wrong width. OH also refuses SL_IID_BUFFERQUEUE and Android's
    simple buffer queue, offering only SL_IID_OH_BUFFERQUEUE, a pull model.
    Mindustry's audio engine was refused a player, ignored the refusal and
    called through the null interface.
    
    opensles_android_adapter.c interposes slCreateEngine from the preloaded
    shim, so the app only ever sees wrappers with Android's types: objects,
    engine, play and volume interfaces, and Android's buffer queue implemented
    over OH's pull callback (app buffers held until consumed, the app's
    callback fired per consumed buffer, silence on underrun). Data sources and
    sinks are rebuilt in OH's layout. Unwrapped interfaces are handed back raw
    and logged by ID. SL_IID_ANDROIDSIMPLEBUFFERQUEUE moves here from the shim;
    the shim is now linked from both files.
    
    On the board Mindustry now logs "pcm: 2 ch, 44100000 mHz, 16/16 bit" and
    CreateAudioPlayer -> 0, and gets past audio to a hwui abort
    ("drawRenderNode called on a context with no surface"): its GLSurfaceView
    shares the activity's window. Aegis, AnkiDroid, AntennaPod, Markor,
    NewPipe, OONI Probe, Termux and Wikipedia still render.
