# Historical execution recipes

These four small sources are preserved byte-for-byte from the outer's private persistent directory. Their full SHA/size and portable origin are in source-snapshots.json. Actual apply and rollback generated swap.sh files and plans/results are retained elsewhere in this package.

They are historical, one-off execution evidence, **not a portable deployment framework or permission to rerun**. They depend on the original repository tools, board lock, exact process/boot/source identities and this window's evidence paths. The original scripts are not rewritten or sanitized; inspection found no hardcoded personal home paths. The r5 and probe-r2 hashes match their pre-execution reviews. The other two are exact current snapshots supplied by the outer; per-invocation source timestamps are not invented.

Large stderr/hilog/crash originals remain in private persistent storage, bound by SHA and portable paths.
