# Task Contract: N7 offline owner routing and shim candidate

## Intent
Route six Flutter packages through the existing U5 window adapter and integrate the accepted bionic shims.

## Decisions
- Identify actual U5 library owners from app-scoped maps before selecting the wrapper.
- Reuse resident HWUI window conversion and platform EGL/GLES implementations; global exports alone are insufficient.
- Copy oc-t4's two shim sources, retain existing exports and compare N1/N2 findings to U5.

Allowed changes:
- benchmark/2026-10-01-n7-native/**
- bms/src/adapter/framework/appspawn-x/bionic_compat/src/strerror_r_compat.cpp
- bms/src/adapter/framework/appspawn-x/bionic_compat/src/dlopen_ext_compat.cpp

Forbidden:

- Claiming ELF exports prove runtime binding or rendering

## Completion Criteria
Scenario: Explicit dispatch and unchanged controls
  Test:
    Filter: n7_dispatch
  Given U5 app-scoped maps and resident wrapper symbols
  When direct EGL calls and proc-address calls use the candidate
  Then window calls reach the supplied wrapper and unrelated calls reach the supplied platform owner
  And control domains retain their previous configuration

Scenario: Accepted bionic additions
  Test:
    Filter: n7_bionic
  Given the two accepted oc-t4 sources
  When compiling and testing the candidate
  Then both exports exist and previous exports remain available

Scenario: Source-bound candidate gates
  Test:
    Filter: n7_package
  Given an offline candidate with exact payload and source hashes
  When N1 and N2 are compared with signed U5
  Then new findings are listed separately and unapproved findings still reject deployment
  And device binding rendering and duplicate outcomes remain unverified

Scenario: Realm zlib alias
  Test:
    Filter: n7_zlib
  Given the accepted r109c 1904-byte alias
  When Realm resolves libz.so in the Westlake native domain
  Then the alias declares SONAME libz.so and NEEDED libshared_libz.z.so
  And no OH system library bytes or SONAME are changed
